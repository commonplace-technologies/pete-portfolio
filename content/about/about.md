Pete is a producer, multi-instrumentalist, and amateur sound designer located in Boston, MA, where he is currently a student at Berklee College of Music.

He has experience in live performance on bass and guitar, and extensive home studio recording experience. He and Louis Davison co-produce a variety of styles for artists in the Boston area.

On his own time, he explores the vast realm of sound design and has worked on Foley and music for indie short films.

Pete is a member of the local band Vanguard, plays bass for YESH, an up-and-coming artist from London, and is actively gigging in the Boston area.
